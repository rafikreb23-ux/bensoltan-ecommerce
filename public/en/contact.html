Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common inquiries

What types of wood do you import?

We import beech, oak, pine, mahogany, abachi and engineered wood boards (MDF, HDF, plywood) from certified European mills.

Do you sell per piece or wholesale only?

We mainly sell wholesale, but small orders for contractors and craftsmen can be accommodated. Contact us for details.

Do you deliver to all Tunisian regions?

Yes, we deliver everywhere (Tunis, Sfax, Sousse, Gafsa, Gabès, Bizerte, Le Kef, Jendouba, etc.). Shipping cost depends on quantity and distance.

How can I get a quote?

Message us via the form, email or phone with: the type of wood/material, the required quantity, and your delivery region. We reply within hours.

Is the wood treated and ready to use?

Yes, the wood is dried and treated to European specifications – ready for carpentry, furniture and construction.

What are the payment terms?

Cash, cheque or bank transfer. For large orders: 30% down payment and the balance on delivery. We adapt to the size of the business relationship.
