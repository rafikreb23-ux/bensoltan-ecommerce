About Us

Ben Soltan wholesale trading company – Kasserine, Tunisia. 13 years of experience in timber import and wholesale trading

Our Story

Founded in Kasserine in 2013, Ben Soltan Trading Company began its activity importing the finest wood from European markets.

Thanks to accumulated expertise and strong relationships with trusted suppliers in over 20 European countries, we have become a reference in timber supply (beech, oak, pine, mahogany, abachi) for the Tunisian market.

As demand grew, our business expanded into wholesale trading of building materials and equipment: insulation, solar equipment, sanitary and electrical fittings, carpentry tools – while maintaining the same standards of quality and reliability.
